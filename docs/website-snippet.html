Snippy

A free, native macOS text expander. Lives in your menu bar and turns abbreviations into snippets you've defined.

What Snippy does

Snippets that fit your work

Plain text, rich text, or images. Group them, toggle them, choose how each one fires.

Matches the destination's style

Paste into Mail and your snippet uses Mail's font. Bullets and lists survive. Turn override formatting on per-snippet when you need it.

Plays nicely with macOS

When an abbreviation collides with a system Text Replacement, Snippy takes precedence cleanly. The editor tells you about collisions.

Your clipboard is safe

Snippy borrows the clipboard for the paste, then puts your previous contents right back.

Three steps to install

Download & drag

Open the DMG and drag Snippy to your Applications folder.

Grant Accessibility

On first launch, Snippy asks for Accessibility permission so it can watch your keystrokes. Toggle it on in System Settings.

Add a snippet

Click the menu bar icon → Open Library → make your first snippet. Try it in any app.

How to use Snippy

Open the library

Click Snippy in the menu bar → Open Library. Groups on the left, snippets in the middle, editor on the right.

Create a snippet

Click + at the bottom of the snippet list. Give it an abbreviation like ;sig and the content to paste. Saves automatically.

Type it anywhere

Switch to any text field — Mail, Slack, Notes, your browser — type the abbreviation. Snippy replaces it instantly.

Tips that pay off

  • Prefix abbreviations with a sigil like ; or \ — ;sig never collides with real words; sig might fire inside "design".
  • Pick a trigger style. Auto-expand fires the instant you finish typing the abbreviation (best with sigils). Require Tab/Space waits for a space or tab (best for word-like abbreviations).
  • Override formatting is off by default so snippets paste in the destination's font and size. Turn it on per-snippet for rich signatures or other content you want pixel-perfect.
  • Group snippets by context — work, personal, a specific client — and toggle whole groups off when they don't apply.
  • Sync across Macs: Settings → Storage → Choose Folder, pick a folder in iCloud Drive or Dropbox, point each Mac at the same one.

Built for privacy

Snippy was designed to do less, not more. It runs entirely on your Mac. Your snippets and your keystrokes never leave it.
